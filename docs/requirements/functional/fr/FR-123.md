# FR-123: Delete An Account

A signed-in [Platform Visitor](../../../terminology/glossary.md#platform-visitor) shall be able to delete their own account.

The system shall require the visitor to confirm the deletion by entering the account email address before any data is removed.

Deletion shall be permanent and immediate: the system shall sign the visitor out, remove the [Account Profile](../../../terminology/glossary.md#account-profile) and [Platform Visitor Identity](../../../terminology/glossary.md#platform-visitor-identity), and disconnect every [Calendar Connection](../../../terminology/glossary.md#calendar-connection) together with its provider credentials.

Events the deleted account organized shall remain reachable through their [Event Link](../../../terminology/glossary.md#event-link) and shall keep their other guests' [Event Responses](../../../terminology/glossary.md#event-response), while the events shall have no [Event Owner](../../../terminology/glossary.md#event-owner).
The deleted visitor's own **Event Responses** shall be removed, and friend requests and historical user logs that reference the account shall be removed.

The system shall not retain, recover, or export any deleted account data.
Signing in with the same email after deletion shall create a new account and a new **Platform Visitor Identity**.

Deletion shall be atomic and idempotent: a failed deletion shall leave no partially removed state, and retrying shall not remove additional data.

This requirement does not specify the calendar-provider re-authentication needed to disconnect a connection.
MongoDB and PostgreSQL deletion mechanics remain implementation details.

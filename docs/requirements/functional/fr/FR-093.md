# FR-093: Clear The Slot Selection When Interacting Outside Active Slots

Clicking anywhere outside [Active Slots](../../../terminology/glossary.md#active-slots) shall clear the current [Slot](../../../terminology/glossary.md#slot) selection.

Touch taps outside **Active Slots** shall clear it the same way.

Covered outside areas include the [Sub-grid Gap](../../../terminology/glossary.md#sub-grid-gap) and the collapsed-hours strip.

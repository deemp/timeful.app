# FR-120: Separate Non-Consecutive Projected Date Columns With a Sub-grid Gap

On a [Timed Grid](../../../terminology/glossary.md#timed-grid), adjacent [Projected Date Columns](../../../terminology/glossary.md#projected-date-column) whose [Civil Dates](../../../terminology/glossary.md#civil-date) are not consecutive in the [Display Timezone](../../../terminology/glossary.md#display-timezone) shall be separated by a [Sub-grid Gap](../../../terminology/glossary.md#sub-grid-gap) containing no grid cells.

Adjacent columns for consecutive [Civil Dates](../../../terminology/glossary.md#civil-date) shall have no [Sub-grid Gap](../../../terminology/glossary.md#sub-grid-gap) between them, including when the columns span a daylight saving transition in the [Display Timezone](../../../terminology/glossary.md#display-timezone).

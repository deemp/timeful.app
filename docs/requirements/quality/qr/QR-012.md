# QR-012: Preserve Authorization When Migrating Core Records

## Source

A migration or backfill copies core records from the legacy MongoDB store to PostgreSQL.

## Stimulus

A legacy record carries a guest credential, an owner reference, an account reference, or ambiguous ownership.

## Environment

The migration runs against migrated accounts, events, responses, attendees, signup forms, availability groups, and folders while one authoritative store per record is enforced.

## Artifact

Migrated event, ownership, and [Event Response](../../../terminology/glossary.md#event-response) authorization.

## Response

The system shall preserve authorization already associated with a [Platform Visitor Identity](../../../terminology/glossary.md#platform-visitor-identity) and shall not grant PostgreSQL event, ownership, or response authority from a legacy credential or from ambiguous ownership.
A legacy guest credential, a base [Event Visitor Control Credential (EVCC)](../../../terminology/glossary.md#event-visitor-control-credential-evcc), or an ambiguous reference shall never become an [Event Owner Edit Token](../../../terminology/glossary.md#event-owner-edit-token) or owner association.
Records that cannot be attributed without inference shall be quarantined and reported instead of repaired.

## Response Measure

Authorization tests confirm that an actor holding only a quarantined legacy credential or an ambiguous reference cannot perform a protected mutation on a migrated record.
Reconciliation evidence confirms that every quarantined record is reported with a reason and that authorization associated through a Platform Visitor Identity is preserved.

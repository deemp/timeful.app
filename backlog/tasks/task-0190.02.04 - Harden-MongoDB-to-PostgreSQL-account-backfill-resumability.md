## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
The account backfill must be safe to interrupt and rerun, and must not loop or partially apply an account unit. Its pagination can re-read the same page, its account and identity writes are not applied as one unit, and preflight, partial-resume, and pre-existing-mapping cases are untested. The migration contract requires each unit to be written atomically and repeated runs to avoid duplicates.
<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:END -->

## Acceptance Criteria
<!-- AC:BEGIN -->
- [ ] #1 Rerunning an interrupted backfill resumes without duplicating platform identities or accounts.
- [ ] #2 Pagination always advances and terminates for every source document, including documents with a zero identifier.
- [ ] #3 Each account migration unit is applied atomically with respect to the source cursor.
- [ ] #4 Tests cover no-write preflight mode, a pre-existing platform identity without an account, a pre-existing account, and a repeated or interrupted run.
<!-- AC:END -->
